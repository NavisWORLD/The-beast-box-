# Zeref English foundation 001 (Cory, 2026-11-12 5:51 PM CT)

Owner words: "the talking is what we really need to train", "We have to give zeref perfect English",
then "Yes, build Zeref a big English learning set and train it through the gate".

## What it is
A RECORDED corpus of public English written by people. It is not Zeref's experience and contains no
model-generated text. Data lives on the append-only `zeref-english-corpus` branch, pinned by commit and
sha256 in `evidence/zeref-english-001/manifest.json`.

| Source | Licence | Kept rows | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 Project Gutenberg children's / simple books | Public domain (US), PG header and licence stripped | 8,244 | 5.9 MB |
| Taskmaster-1 (2019) self + WOz dialogs, Google LLC | CC BY 4.0 (attribution in manifest) | 17,803 | 11.7 MB |

Screening: PII (email, URL, phone, SSN-shaped), toxicity (sha256 word blocklist), non-ASCII, exact dedupe.
3% held out by stable hash, never trained. Excluded with reasons in the manifest: TinyStories (GPT-generated),
oasst1 (unsure: pasted model output), DailyDialog and EmpatheticDialogues (non-commercial), Cornell (no licence),
Topical-Chat (CDLA-Sharing), SGD and Simple English Wikipedia (share-alike).

## How it trains
`scripts/zeref_english_mix.py` fills `ZEREF_ENGLISH_SHARE` (0.5) of the existing 007 pretrain pool, rotated per cycle,
books 60 : dialogue 40. The trainer is unchanged: each batch stays 4 game + 12 chat (authored identity lessons,
served shape, Dolly) + 2 pretrain rows, so about one English passage per batch. Gate, pin, eval sets, gear/heart
flags and the 12D work are untouched. One-line revert: `ZEREF_ENGLISH_SHARE: '0'`.

## How clarity is reported
`scripts/zeref_english_clarity.py`, REPORT-ONLY, after publication: held-out English NLL and perplexity on 200 fixed
passages, plus the real-word share of replies to the 8 fixed chat prompts (proxy dictionary `wordlist.txt`).

## Honest limits
A round is 320 updates, so a candidate sees about 640 pretrain rows, roughly 320 of them English. The corpus
(26,047 passages) covers about 80 rounds before it repeats. It is sized for the free runner, and many rounds are
needed before his English is clear.

Owner decision still open: the existing 007 pretrain slice uses TinyStories, which was generated by GPT-3.5/4.
This PR leaves it as it is. Setting the share to 1.0 is not allowed (share < 1); removing TinyStories needs its own decision.
