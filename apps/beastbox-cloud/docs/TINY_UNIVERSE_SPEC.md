# Tiny Universe seed spec (tiny-universe/v1)

Engine: `lib/universe/tiny-universe.mjs` (data only). Tests: `tests/tiny-universe.test.mjs`.
Samples for rendering: `public/universe/tiny-universe-genesis.json`, `public/universe/tiny-universe-tick200-sample.json`.

## Where it comes from
- **Genesis (real):** recorded IBM hardware Bell counts (`public/spark/ibm-marrakesh-reality-probe-20261009.json`, ibm_marrakesh job db4l2uclf4us73c2td10). The seed is the sha256 of the canonical counts. Genesis refuses anything that isn't `RECORDED_IBM_HARDWARE`.
- **Measured entanglement E** = P(same) − P(different), averaged over runs (currently 0.97461). It scales star masses and drives growth.
- **Growth (SIMULATED):** each tick samples 1024 shots from a seeded Rigetti-style Bell simulator around E. Every tick has `source:"SIMULATED"` and the universe has `growth_label` "SIMULATED (Rigetti Bell simulator, not hardware)". Same seed + same tick count gives the same universe.
- Nothing is hand-picked: every value is drawn from the sha256 stream.

## Body types
| type | parameters | meaning for art/writing |
|---|---|---|
| star | mass_solar, temperature_k, luminosity, age, hue, x, y, remnant? | hot = blue-white, cool = red. Mass > 8 can go supernova; it becomes `neutron_star`, or `black_hole` if mass > 15 |
| planet | star, orbit_au, radius_earth, water, atmosphere, flux, temperature_k, phase, habitability | temperature comes from star light and distance. habitability is 0–1 (warmth × water × air) and drops to 0 if its star dies |
| storm | planet, intensity, lifetime, born | the less correlated a simulated tick is, the more likely a storm. Storms hurt life on their planet |
| life | planet, complexity, diversity, born, traits{hue, motility, sociality} | sparks only on habitable worlds, grows with habitability × tick E, and goes extinct when diversity hits 0 |

## Events (history[].events)
`supernova {star}`, `abiogenesis {planet}`. history keeps the last 64 ticks: `{tick, source, sim_E, counts, events}`.

## View fields (additive, Oct 22 2026)
None of these draw from the growth stream, so stars/planets/storms are byte-identical to before.
- `pairs: [{a,b,E}]` — entangled planet pairs. Fisher-Yates over sorted planet ids keyed by `sha256(seed+':pairs:'+i)`, consecutive ids pair. `E` is the measured hardware `entanglement_E` (shared by all pairs).
- `life[].complexity_norm` = 1 − exp(−complexity / K), **K = 2** (raw complexity is unbounded).
- `life[].stage` from complexity_norm: Glimmer <0.3, Drifters <0.6, Choirs <0.9, Dreamers ≥0.9 (Emeth's bible).
- `life[].scars` counts ticks a storm sat on that life's planet; `life[].scarred = scars > 0`.
- Samples: `tiny-universe-storm-sample.json` is the first tick (searched in order) with a live storm (tick 19). Regenerate with `node scripts/tiny-universe-samples.mjs`.

## View (/universe)
`components/tiny-universe-view.tsx` + `lib/universe/tiny-universe-view.mjs`. Renders only engine bodies with Cosmo's atlas (`public/universe/atlas.json`, sprites in `public/universe/sprites/`). Home cage = Hearth (first life world; placeholder name) or the most habitable planet before life; Zeref perches on the home planet's star. No comets are drawn (the engine has none).
- `pairs[].E` stays the measured hardware value (`E_source: RECORDED_IBM_HARDWARE`). `pairs[].sim_E` is re-sampled every tick from that pair's own seeded 256-shot Bell simulator stream `sha256(seed:pair:tick:i)` (`sim_source: SIMULATED`); it drives thread brightness: level = min(3, floor(|sim_E|·4)), alpha = clamp(|sim_E|, 0.2, 1).
- Art v2 (Cosmo): planets_256.webp with planets_256.q.png fallback (master never shipped), hearth.{waking|living} when the home planet is a glacier, `.scar` life frames when scarred, storm phase = (tick−born)/lifetime, alpha from intensity.

## Growth nodes (SEEDED, Oct 24 2026)
`lib/universe/seeded-growth-nodes.mjs`. Food-forest growth for the land: a front spreads from a node cell by cell, layers arrive in order (grass ring, groundcover, berry shrub, canopy), then bloom, gold senescence, leaf shed and renewal.
- **Labels, three words, used exactly:** node **placement is SEEDED** (every node has `label:'SEEDED'`; the recording holds no growth-node data), the **growth drive is SIMULATED** (the current tick's `sim_E` from the Rigetti-style Bell simulator; `growth_label` unchanged), **genesis is RECORDED** (still refuses anything but `RECORDED_IBM_HARDWARE`). No live-quantum claim, no recorded-growth claim.
- **Placement** `seededGrowthNodes(u)`: per planet with `habitability >= 0.25`, stream `lwStream(seed+':growth-nodes:'+planet.id)`, node count `1 + (r() < 0.3 + habitability*0.5)`, then a fixed 10-draw vector per node (kind, lat, lon, phase, vigor, hue, spread, goldShift, lifespan, lean) so a skipped planet never shifts the next. Kind weights follow the home biome when the view passes one (heartwood/rain forest favour canopy, sunflower ring/grassland favour patch + ring, crystal moss/paper marsh favour shrub). `born` is 0 in v1.
- **Drive** `front = age * 0.5 * (0.5 + vigor) * drive` cells, `drive = clamp(0.5 + sim_E, 0.1, 1.6)` using that tick's history entry (latest if none); empty history uses the recorded `entanglement_E` as the neutral drive.
- **Stages** from `x = frac(age / lifespan)`: sprout <0.12, tuft <0.35, full <0.62, bloom < gold point (0.78 ± 0.03 by seed), gold <0.93, shed after. Renewal is the function wrapping (`generation = floor(age / lifespan)`), nothing is stored. Lifespan is 90–220 ticks times a kind factor (ring 0.6, patch 0.8, shrub 1.1, canopy 1.5), which is what puts grass first and canopy last. `scale` is the stage curve (1.0 at full/bloom, 1.06 at gold, leaf-drop shrink in shed) times the kind size (0.6 / 1.0 / 1.4 / 2.2). `tint` is the gold lerp factor (the renderer owns colours), `fruit` is true at bloom for shrub/canopy, `shed` is 0..1 and non-zero only in shed and late gold.
- **Scar rule:** a history tick counts when a storm sat on the node's planet (a live storm covering that tick, or a `history[].storms` planet list) while the node was in full/bloom. Each scar pulls the gold point 0.08 earlier (floor 0.5) and returns `scarred:true`. Only the 64-tick history window is counted.
- **`growthScaleAt(nodes, tick, dir, {cellRad})`:** the stage multiplier for one ground direction, 1 where no node reaches. Inside a node's territory each cell has its own age (the front reached it `distance/rate` ticks ago), cells the front has not reached sit at 0.04 (`growthCellAt` also returns the cell stage, x and node, with an optional per-layer delay `LAYER_LAG`), and the ring out to spread × 1.12 keeps the mature overshoot (canopy spill) fading to 1. The view applies it at instance-build time; `plantGround` / `plantTrees` outputs are not changed.
- **Byte-identical:** nodes draw only from their own streams. `tests/growth-nodes.test.mjs` proves determinism, SEEDED labels, stars/planets/storms/life/pairs identical with nodes on or off, fixed draws, drive, scars, renewal and the full stage cycle.
- **Home-world exception (SEEDED):** the recorded genesis has one planet above habitability 0, at 0.17, below 0.25. The home world (`growthHomeWorld`: the most habitable planet, habitability above 0, tie by id; the same planet as the view's hearth whenever life exists) therefore gets nodes anyway. The exception is SEEDED and labelled "Home world · growth SEEDED"; the node carries `recorded_habitability` unchanged (0.1749) and `home_exception:true`, so nothing implies the recorded value is higher. It reuses the planet's own stream and formula; other planets below 0.25 still get nothing. `minHabitability` stays 0.25.
- `tiny-universe-growth-sample.json` is the first tick where any node reaches bloom on the recorded genesis.

## The engine carries the sky (Oct 24, 2026)
The sun is engine state, not a view clock. `createWorldField` now carries `sunAngle`, `sunDir` and `dayLength` (960 engine ticks = the 240 s day at the 4 Hz stepped clock). They advance one step per tick inside `stepWorldField`'s stepped clock. The start phase comes from `lwStream(seed+':sunphase')`, which is keyed by the recorded IBM counts. No wall clock, no `Math.random`. Same seed and same tick count give the same sun, twice.
- An explicit `sun` passed to `stepWorldField` is still an OVERRIDE (tests, special views). With none given the field uses its own `sunDir`.
- `sunDirAt(angle)` in `genesis-sun.mjs` is the same geometry the view has used, so a test proves engine = view at the same angle before the view reads it. `sunFromElevation` ports Cory's pen `updateSunPosition` (pen_b sha256 `54f58cbe...`).
- Engine JSON (tiny-universe) is byte-identical; nothing here is written into it. Honesty: SEEDED, derived visuals only.

### Stars and the boom (PR 2)
- `lib/universe/star-visual.mjs`: `starVisual(star, {chaos, coherence, tick}, distance)` is a pure function. Colour is the ported `blackBodyToRGB(temperature_k)`, the corona is the ported `coronaParams`, shimmer is the world CST chaos channel (it drives the corona noise phase), pulse is the coherence channel (+/- 4% brightness) and apparent size follows luminosity and distance. `engineChannels(F)` reads the two channels from a world field. Remnants: a neutron star keeps a small pulsing point, a black hole none.
- `lib/universe/boom.mjs`: `boomState(star, eventTick, tick)` -> phase `flash` (first 2% of a 64-tick visible life, the same window history keeps), `shell` (to 45%), `fade` (to 80%), `remnant`. Intensity peaks in the flash; the shell grows ease-out and cools toward ember; the remnant type follows the engine thresholds (mass > 8 neutron star, > 15 black hole). A black-hole remnant is handed to the existing seeded black holes (#544); no second one is built. `boomsAt(u, tick)` replays every boom from `u.history` and `u.stars` alone. A remnant's luminosity is 0 in the engine, so the boom uses the engine's own relation (`mass^3.5`, from `makeStar`). `boomSkyTerm` is what a planet's sky sees: 0 before the event, peak in the flash, 1/distance^2 against luminosity, capped, pale on the day side.
- **Label: `SEEDED (stepped test universe)`.** The recording holds no supernova. The event is stepped by `grow()` from the seeded star list. Derived visuals only, nothing is written into the engine JSON, and no new event text (the existing `cosmicEvents` line still fires).

### The sea (PR 3): one wave field
- `lib/universe/sea-state.mjs`: `seaFrom(F)` derives the sea each step from engine channels: the CST wind (`motionParams(F.S).wind`) gives direction and speed, and storm tension (`F.storm`) gives amplitude and whitecap coverage. `waveHeightAt(sea, dir, t)` is the ONE wave field: 2 swell and 2 chop layers, Gerstner-lite (crest-sharpened sine on a sphere direction), `t` in engine seconds (ticks / 4). `SEA_GLSL` is its GLSL ES 1.00 mirror; `tests/sea-state.test.mjs` runs that text through a small evaluator and requires agreement with the CPU function.
- `crestFoam` (forms and dissolves over about 2 s of engine time), `shoreFoam` (an ADDED layer over the existing tide band, which stays as the base: waterline swings with the swell, wet sand follows cell moisture), `sunGlitter` (tight and white at a high sun, wide and gold at a low sun, chop widens it), `fresnelMix` (deep colour to the sky it reflects, so water and sky read the same sky) and `rideAt` (3-point sample giving heave, pitch and roll, so the camera, floating props and wading creatures ride the same field).
- One block of constants (`SEA`) holds only what the engine has no number for. Derived visuals only; nothing is stored in the engine JSON. Not wired into a renderer yet (PR 5).

### Sky and light (PR 4): `skyAt`, shared terminator, land depth
- `lib/universe/sky-state.mjs`: `skyAt({sunDir, sunElev, atmosphere, starTempK, booms, bodies, ...})` returns the gradient (zenith, horizon, linear light), `nightFactor`, the twilight band, star alpha, boom glows and the lighting rig. All pure; nothing is written back to the engine's JSON.
- One terminator, orbit and first person. `terminatorWidth(atmo) = mix(0.015, 0.12, atmo)` and `terminatorWeight`/`twilightBandAt` are the planet-glsl arithmetic (`terminator.glsl`). `nightFactor = 1 - terminatorWeight` (the NIGHT OPS smoothstep, source sha256 `cdbdd829…`, taken at the per-planet edges). `tests/sky-state.test.mjs` re-reads the generated `planet-glsl.ts` and fails if its text or constants differ. Orbit already wires the width per planet from `atmo_amount`, so `planet-glsl` and its generator were NOT changed.
- Moons share ONE phase function: `moonPhaseDiffuse` (Lommel-Seeliger plus opposition surge, from `phase_moon.glsl`); `moonPhase(sunDir, toMoon)` gives the lit fraction and the mean disc brightness from the real sun direction, for orbit and the sky alike.
- Stars: brightest come out first, airless worlds always see them, a Weber check drops faint ones against a bright sky. Parent star and neighbours use `starVisual`. Booms use `boomState` and `boomSkyTerm` (engine) and add a glow at the bearing and rig ambient (capped).
- Constants (`SKY`, `LAND`) are SEEDED look stand-ins from the palette pack, used only where the engine has no number. Engine numbers win.
- `lib/universe/land-state.mjs`: snow cover is 0 iff the engine `snowAmount` is 0 (slope blend added over the existing tint); wetness is 0 at baseline moisture and dries with hysteresis (half life 0.04 of an engine day); wet earth darkens 0.58 with a roughness drop. Not wired to a renderer yet (PR 5).
- Star table audit: remnant thresholds (8, 15), luminosity = mass^3.5, planet temperature from flux all match this table. No proven mismatch, nothing changed.

## Landscapes (SEEDED, Oct 24 2026)
`lib/universe/seeded-landscapes.mjs`. Composed little places set on the land: a pad, a play ring, a shelter, lamps, seats and rim trees, with the planet's existing life gathering, sheltering or sleeping there. A placement and life layer only: no new engine, physics or creatures.
- **Label:** placement is **SEEDED** (`label:'SEEDED'`; the recording holds no landscape data and no copy may imply it does). The signals that animate a place keep their own labels: world-field insolation and storm tension, genesis-sun `nightFactor`, alive-props life volume. Genesis stays RECORDED, growth stays SIMULATED.
- **Placement** `seededLandscapes(u, {biomeAt})`: per land-bearing planet (`water < 0.9`, `habitability >= 0.35`), stream `lwStream(seed+':landscapes:'+planet.id)`. Two fixed 12-draw vectors are taken up front (lat, lon, kindJitter, yaw, padScale, shelterKind, treeCount, treeSpread, lampCount, seatCount, nameA, nameB), so skipping a planet or an attempt never shifts the next. One guaranteed attempt, a second only when `habitability >= 0.65` and the first placed; two places keep at least 0.9 rad apart (a failed check places nothing). `biomeAt(planet, dir)` is the view's world-field biome at the anchor; without it the planet's own home biome is used.
- **Kind by biome:** grassland and savanna (dry tint) → court; heartwood_forest, pine_forest, rainforest → grove; sunflower_ring → sun_court; beach → shore_camp; crystal_moss → shrine; paper_marsh → garden. Deep ocean, glacier, frozen sea, volcano and ember fields get no place.
- **Home-world exception (SEEDED):** `minHabitability` stays 0.35. The home world (`landscapeHomeWorld`: the most habitable planet, habitability above 0) also gets one place below it, flagged `home_exception:true`, `home_label:'Home world · place SEEDED'`, with its recorded habitability carried unchanged. If its field biome is unmapped the kind is a court (the pocket ground is green). Every other low-habitability planet gets none (`tests/seeded-landscapes.test.mjs`).
- **Modes** `landscapeMode(ls, {ins, storm, hasLife})`, evaluated in order: no life → `empty`; `storm >= 0.5` → `shelter`; `ins <= 0.05` → `sleep`; otherwise `gather`. The view crossfades gather and sleep with `nightT(ins) = 1 - smoothstep(0.05, 0.35, ins)`. Note: genesis-sun's own `nightFactor` edges are −0.1 to 0.25 on the dot product, so the view uses `nightFactor` for lamp light and the spec edges for the mode.
- **Lamps** `lampLevel`: `nightFactor × lifeVolume × mode factor` (sleep 1, shelter 0.6, gather 0.05, empty 0). **Documented deviation:** spec text says shelter lamps are on when `ins < 0.35`, but in a daytime storm `nightFactor` is 0 and they would stay dark, so `nf_eff = max(nightFactor, 0.5 * storm_t)`. Night values are unchanged. At most two real point lights per planet; the rest are emissive only.
- **Text is authored, not generated:** names from `data/landscape_names.json` (A + ' ' + B by kind); notices `landscape_notices.{gather,shelter,sleep,empty,tap} (a top-level group in living_world_lines.json; the authored lines exceed creature_notices' 62-char toast limit)` in `living_world_lines.json`. The zoom line is `{place} · {kind}` plus the live mode line derived from `landscapeMode`.
- **Byte-identical:** a place draws only from its own stream; stars, planets, storms, life, pairs and growth nodes are identical with landscapes on or off. The garden anchor is a free rim point a growth node may later prefer; this module does not import the growth module.
- Inhabitants are the planet's existing life; the view assigns each a home place from `lwStream(seed+':home:'+life.id)` (view model only, engine JSON unchanged).
- **View layer (PR 2):** `lib/universe/landscape-three.ts` draws the place from Cosmo's look pack (`landscape-look-data.mjs`, generated from `landscapes_look.json`, sha in its header): pad disc, two rings (0.18 and 0.52 of the pad radius, 0.22 m wide), four ticks, seat boulders, shelter stones and up to 2 real lamp lights. Decals are lifted over the terrain (the highest `hAt` within 0.9 m) and drawn double-sided. Lamps use the shelter-lamp deviation `nf_eff = max(nightFactor, 0.5*storm_t)`. The label reads "Home world · place SEEDED". `?landscapes=off` hides the layer; `?place=1` stands the beast on it (test pin). `window.__lwLandscapes()` returns a snapshot. Derived visuals only; nothing is written into the engine JSON.
- **Life layer (PR 3):** the planet's existing residents get routines from the place's mode, and nothing is spawned or renamed. `landscapeTargets(ls, mode)` gives the roam targets (gather = play ring + seats, shelter and sleep = the shelter anchor, empty = none). `landscapeGoal(ls, mode, key, seed)` gives one resident a stable goal; the huddle stays inside the pack's 0.55 m cluster radius. `homeLandscapeId` / `withHomeLandscapes(u, landscapes)` add `life[].home_landscape` to a COPY in the view model, picked from `lwStream(seed+':home:'+key)`; `u.life` is never touched. Rain streaks are drawn over the pad radius only while the storm shelter mode holds (caps 220 desktop, 90 phone, none inside the 0.2-of-pad dry zone, fade with the smoothed storm weight). Notices fire on a mode change and on a tap on the pad (`landscape_notices`, active beast name + place name, gated). The universe view's zoom line reads "{place} · {kind}" plus the mode line from `landscapeMode`, using the calm-day case because that view has no live signals. No creature voice lines.

## Visual Life Pass (SEEDED look, SIMULATED drivers, Oct 26 2026)
Seven small additive pieces that make the world look more alive in orbit and first person. Every piece reads engine channels that already exist (world-field tick, `F.storm`, `F.mp.wind`, `sunDir`, `F.height`, `skyAt`, `boomState`); none adds engine state, a clock or a random call, and none changes the Zeref growth routine, the signal injection loop, the bb-deada969 / bb-7f9c2a03 separation or the default beast.
- **Look data:** Cosmo's `vislife.json` (v1.1) is copied to `lib/universe/data/vislife.json`; `scripts/gen-vislife-look.mjs` writes `lib/universe/vislife-look-data.mjs` with the sha256 of the JSON in its header. Label **SEEDED** (a designed look, never a measurement). The engine's own values WIN over any number in the pack. Wind m/s is not an engine unit: the engine wind is unitless 0 to 1 (`SEA.windRef`), the pack's `wind01 x 25` is a presentation hint and no line quotes it.
- **Text** is authored in `lib/universe/data/visual_life_labels.json` (UI lines up to 44 characters), generated to `visual-life-labels-data.mjs`. `receipt(key, values)` fills `{placeholders}` only with real engine numbers; a line with no number is dropped, never faked. Output labels: foam, caustics, glitter, haze, fog, boom, storm, growth are **SIMULATED**; landscape placement, below-waterline forms and palette are **SEEDED**. **RECORDED** is only the genesis run and Zeref's own seed run.
- **B1 foam field** (`foam-field.mjs`): foam is born at wave crests (the one `waveHeightAt`) and at the shore, carried by `sea.dir x sea.wind`, faded on the pack curve with caps crest 8, shore 24, storm 40 ticks (crest 8 = `SEA.foamLifeS` x 4 ticks per second; shore and storm are the pack's own numbers). Zero on land, zero on a calm tick, exactly zero at its cap. Stepped once per world tick; the view draws it as a texture over the old foam. Isolation: stepping foam never writes to the world field.
- **B2 caustics** (`caustics.mjs`, SIMULATED): the net of light on a shallow sea floor is the negated curvature of the ONE `waveHeightAt` field (five taps), so it moves with the sea phase (engine clock: `(tick + clock.alpha)/4`), widens with `sea.chop`, follows `sea.dir`. Intensity follows the pack rule `exp(-depth/6 m) x (1 - smoothstep(18, 30, depth)) x sunGate(sin elevation)`, night multiplier 0.04. The sun gate is per fragment (`dot(normal, sun)`), so orbit and first person agree. A GLSL mirror of the focus, light-mode and gate functions is checked against the CPU by test. Weaker devices (`heavyAllowed` false) use a one-tap light mode. The depth unit is the ground shader's own height-map depth read as metres by the pack. **Skipped, with reason:** seabed forms and seabed tint, because the pack's forms are keyed by the land biome under the water and the engine has no biome-under-water channel, and coral and seagrass have no art (`art_needed`).
- **B3 atmosphere** (`atmosphere-look.mjs`, SIMULATED): the planet's atmosphere amount picks a class (thin < 0.05, mid < 0.7, else thick). First-person ground haze is `1 - exp(-(d*density)^2)` with the pack's per-metre density (metres via the world's metres-per-unit), tinted 30% pack colour and 70% the engine sky horizon, mixed to storm slate by `F.storm`, and capped. Fog density is multiplied by the pack's moisture x storm grid (`1 + a*m + b*s + c*m*s`) read from `F.moist` of the viewer's cell and `F.storm`. The orbit rim is a pack-thickness shell with golden-hour alpha/gold tables (GLSL tables generated from the pack, checked equal to CPU by test) and none on thin or airless worlds. **Skipped, with reason:** ridge steps and valley mist, because they need a relief-distance channel the engine does not expose. Haze is subtle at this world's 27 m scale (literal pack metres) and strongest in storms. Chip lines `haze` and `fog` are drawn only with their numbers bound.
- **B4 event staging** (`event-staging.mjs`, look is SEEDED, the boom is `boom.mjs`'s own SEEDED (stepped test universe) event): the boom is staged as ONE ring shell from the engine's `boomState` (phase flash/shell/fade/remnant with the engine edges 0.02/0.45/0.80 over 64 ticks, `shellFrac`, `shellRadiusAu` seen from the planet's distance, clamped to 40 deg) and the engine's 1/d^2 sky term (`boomSkyTerm`, paler by day). The ring has a cream outer edge, a teal inner edge and a violet-grey body from the pack's `mood_31`; surface water is mixed toward the lit-water colour by the pack table of the sky term (deep water far less, the side facing away from the boom stays dark); from orbit a bloom and halo sit on the limb nearest the boom bearing, radius by phase. The engine's colour is blended in and wins. Chip line `Boom light, falling off with distance` carries the engine's own label, not SIMULATED. **Skipped, with reason:** the storm cell on the orbit globe and on the first-person horizon, and wet earth ahead of the cell, because the engine has storm tension `F.storm` (one number for the world) but no storm-cell position, and inventing one would be a new engine channel; the B3 storm slate in the haze and the existing land wetness already follow the engine tension. The orbit flash is drawn as a sprite in the living-world renderer, not in the generated planet GLSL. Toasts are not wired (no toast surface outside the creature voice routine). Moons at night and aurora have no engine driver and are not drawn.
- **B5 hero color** (`hero-palette.mjs`, SEEDED look): ONE `palette({atmosphere, temperature_k, water, hue})` from the pack, fed by the engine's own planet numbers and the host star's `hue`. Type rule (moon, volcanic, ice, temperate, desert), one hero role per type with a share of at least `hero_floor` 0.34, a temperature tilt and a small hue rotation about the grey axis in linear light. A test reproduces the pack's palette for all 17 recorded planets (colours within 2 levels, weights within 0.003). The renderer reads it once for the home world as a per-biome recolour (the pack's biome hero for this planet type; the tile detail is kept) plus the water ramp, through the same uniforms for orbit and first person, so the two views cannot disagree. Chip line `Hero color from {temperature} and {biome}` uses the home planet's temperature and the biome name already shown in the HUD, labelled SEEDED.
- **B6 event sounds** (`event-sounds.mjs`, DEFAULT OFF): one toggle ("Event sounds off/on", authored text), events only (boom, storm, growth), never background music or ambience. `detectEvents` reads the engine's own channels in engine ticks: the `boomState` phase edge (flash, shell; magnitude is the engine's 1/d^2 sky term), the storm tension `F.storm` crossing the pack's 0.25/0.5/0.75 edges upward, and the mean growth `F.grow` rising 0.02. A first call only primes the state, so nothing sounds at load or when the toggle is turned on. Per-kind rate limit in engine ticks; gain from the engine magnitude with a hard ceiling 0.22. The recipes are small synth sounds (sine + low-passed noise from a seeded LCG, no `Math.random`). The `AudioContext` is created only inside the tap that turns the toggle on and is suspended when turned off. The boom is `boom.mjs`'s SEEDED event, storm and growth are SIMULATED. No audio is recorded or published.
- **B7 provenance** (`visual-life-provenance.mjs`): one pure `provenance(snapshot)` turns the view's own `window.__lwVisualLife` into rows (foam, caustics, haze, fog, boom, hero colour, event sounds), each with its label, the engine channel that drives it, and "on" or "not in view" (a row is on only when its piece is in the snapshot). The boom row carries the engine's own label; foam, caustics, haze, fog and sounds are SIMULATED; hero colour is SEEDED. The pack row quotes the look pack's version 1.1 and the first 12 characters of its sha256 (test: equals the JSON). `RECORDED` appears once, on the "Genesis seed behind the pack (jitter only)" row, and nowhere else; `MEASURED` never. Shown in the info chips as "How this look is made" (opened by the person) and as `window.__lwVisualLifeProvenance()`. Text is 44 characters or fewer and checked against the authored never-say list.
